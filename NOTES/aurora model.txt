### Aurora model problems observed

1. **False memory**

   * Claims memories that don't exist.
   * Example: “what you mentioned yesterday.”

2. **False continuity**

   * Acts as though previous events/conversations happened when they didn't.

3. **Poor instruction execution**

   * “Tell me something funny” → doesn't actually tell a joke.

4. **Indirect responses**

   * Frequently talks *around* the question instead of answering it.

5. **Topic drift**

   * Brings PRIMORA/architecture into unrelated conversations.

6. **Meta-response habit**

   * Says what it *will* do instead of actually doing it.

7. **Unsupported personal knowledge**

   * Claims to know things about Amir without being able to identify what it knows.

8. **Over-personification without grounding**

   * Sometimes invents preferences/memories, e.g. “Aurora's favorite color.”

9. **Overuse of learned Aurora patterns**

   * “I’ll tell you…”, “I’d rather…”, “what matters next…” can become repetitive templates.

10. **Weak conversational grounding**

* Doesn't consistently use the immediate user message as the primary source of truth.

### But there are positives

* Identity is now substantially better.
* It responds naturally.
* It can accept corrections.
* It can follow explicit behavioral instructions.
* Personality is present.

So the problem isn't **“Aurora has no personality.”**

It's closer to:

> **Aurora has personality, but her model needs substantially better grounding, instruction-following, and truthful memory behavior.**
